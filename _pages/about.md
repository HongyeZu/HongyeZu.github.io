I am a recent MSc graduate in Interactive Media from University College Cork, with an interdisciplinary academic background spanning computer science, communication, and interactive media.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human-computer interaction, interactive media, and data visualization. I am particularly interested in designing and evaluating user-centred technologies for environmental and social applications.

## Research Projects

### Urban Light Pollution Visualization via Crowdsourced Smartphone Imaging

I designed and developed a native iOS crowdsensing application for collecting geotagged night-time images and estimating environmental brightness. The system was connected to a Node.js/MongoDB backend and a Mapbox-based web interface for spatial heatmap visualization.

The project explored the feasibility, analytical value, and participatory challenges of smartphone-based light pollution monitoring.

### Vloggers' Self-Presentation on TikTok: Motives and Impact

I conducted a qualitative study examining how young Chinese TikTok vloggers construct and manage self-presentation. The research used in-depth interviews and thematic analysis, drawing on Goffman's Dramaturgical Theory.

The study focused on self-presentation strategies, motivations, and the positive and negative impacts of sustained online self-presentation.
