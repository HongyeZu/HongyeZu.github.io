## Research Experience

### Urban Light Pollution Visualization via Crowdsourced Smartphone Imaging

- Designed and developed a native iOS crowdsensing application using Swift and AVCaptureSession for capturing night-time images with automatic GPS tagging, timestamping, and on-device brightness estimation.
- Developed a Node.js/MongoDB backend and a Mapbox GL JS web interface for spatial heatmap visualization.
- Piloted the system in Harbin, China, to investigate the feasibility, spatiotemporal analytical value, and participatory challenges of smartphone-based light pollution monitoring.
- Conducted an 8-participant usability study, achieving a mean ease-of-use rating of 4.6/5 and an average task completion time of 28 seconds.
- Evaluated end-to-end system performance across 20 full-cycle tests, from image capture and upload to visualization.

### Vloggers' Self-Presentation on TikTok: Motives and Impact

- Conducted a qualitative study applying Goffman's Dramaturgical Theory to examine self-presentation among young Chinese TikTok vloggers.
- Designed and conducted in-depth interviews with participants and applied thematic analysis to examine self-presentation methods, motivations, and audience impact.
- Identified themes related to stage-shaping behaviours, social connection, monetization, self-realization, and the positive and negative impacts of sustained self-presentation.
- Used iterative cross-checking of interview data, theoretical literature, and comparative analysis across participants.
